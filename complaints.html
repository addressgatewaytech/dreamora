3. Your consumer rights

As a consumer in Qatar you are protected by Law No. (8) of 2008 on Consumer Protection and its implementing regulations, including the right to accurate information, clear prices in Qatari Riyals, an invoice for your purchase, and services that match what was agreed.

4. Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Consumer Protection Department of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, State of Qatar, through its official channels.
